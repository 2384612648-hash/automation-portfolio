# Nocturne artwork

`robot-hand.webp` and `piano-prism.webp` are conceptual images generated with OpenAI image_gen for ZERO on 2026-11-05. They depict a titanium robot hand balancing a smoky glass sphere and a piano with a rose glass prism; they are not photographs of the owner's equipment. Palette: graphite, pewter, dusty rose, ivory; no text or logos.

`rose-orbit-atlas.webp` and `data-prism-atlas.webp` are original 3D shader renders: 48 frames, 24 fps, two-second cycles. They were rendered against green, keyed to RGBA, and packed as 6 columns × 8 rows with 192px square frames. Page controls scrub these prerendered frames; only the ambient light field is real-time WebGL.

All four assets are served locally. No remote image, font, icon or video service is required at runtime. Hero images total about 66 KB; both transparent atlases total about 406 KB.
